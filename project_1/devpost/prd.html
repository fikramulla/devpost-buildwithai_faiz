Look and feel

Clean and simple. Light green and light yellow, readable gray lettering, nature and fresh-air imagery, and cartoons representing each activity. Friendly language welcomes another attempt.

The brand is confirmed. Final tagline and typography remain open design details.

Explore each behavior

First-use setup

Ask how many cigarettes the person currently smokes per day. Suggest 75%, rounded up, and allow adjustment.

Check: 20 → 15; 10 → 8. The chosen daily limit is visible.

Input safeguard: whole numbers of at least 1; invalid input receives a clear message. Zero-limit goals need a separate design.

Daily counter and circle

Minus on the left, count in the middle, plus on the right. Each click adjusts by one; the circle reads, for example, “10 of 15 today.”

The support threshold is two-thirds of the limit, rounded up.

Check: changing the count updates the label, color, and support. Counts above the limit remain accurate and can still be logged.

Input safeguard: minus is disabled at zero. Reopening on the same browser/device retains the current day's count and limit.

Craving activities

A small collection covers breathing, gentle stretches or yoga, and walking where practical. Each choice opens a short summary and matching cartoon.

Check: at the threshold, support becomes prominent. Choosing an activity shows instructions without celebrating completion yet.

Approved behavior: activities are also accessible below the threshold, and can be closed without completing them. Final instructions need appropriate sourcing and review.

Completion celebration

“I did it” produces “Great work—you’re on your way!” and short confetti. Return to the tracker afterward.

Check: no cigarette is subtracted. Reduced-motion users receive the message without animation.

Daily check-in and reset

Each new day begins at zero cigarettes. Show the previous chosen limit and ask “How are you doing? Would you like to change your limit?”

Check: proceed with the previous limit or change it; changing the limit updates the threshold.

Confirmed in product review

  • Remember today's count and chosen limit when reopening on the same browser/device.
  • Keep activities available below the threshold; make them more prominent at it.
  • Prevent negative counts; accept whole-number inputs and limits of at least 1. Defer zero-limit goals.
  • Keep accounts, cross-device sync, external notifications, and a history dashboard outside this version.

These behaviors and exclusions were approved by the learner during product review.

Boundary: now and later

Now: setup, daily counter/circle, illustrated activity support, celebration, daily reset/check-in, and a demonstrable web app with deployment as a learner goal.

Later: savings, quitting-product information, broader health resources, and a more extensive quit plan.
