Small steps toward a smoke-free day.

A calm web app that pairs daily cigarette tracking with practical craving support—and celebrates completing a helpful alternative.

Approved · ready for product definition

Read the canonical Markdown scope

Who this helps

A smoker trying to quit through manageable changes, who needs accessible tools and alternatives during the day—including at a work desk.

What makes it useful

The count leads to an action: as someone approaches their limit, the app brings forward friendly support and a brief alternative activity.

The core loop

For someone taking gradual steps toward quitting:

  1. Set & logChoose a daily limit and record cigarettes.
  2. See & respondCheck the count; craving support comes forward at 10 and above.
  3. Try an alternativeFollow a short breathing, stretching, yoga, or walking activity.
  4. Complete & celebrateTap “I did it” for encouragement and confetti. Return throughout the day.

Proof in the demo: logging updates the circle, the support appears, and completing an activity triggers the celebration.

The agreed example: a daily limit of 15

Show an explicit label such as “10 of 15 today” so a filling circle is understood as a cigarette count.

What belongs in this experiment

Now · the working proof of concept
  • User-chosen daily limit, cigarette logging, and a labeled colored circle.
  • Friendly craving support and a small set of guided workplace-friendly activities.
  • An “I did it” completion action, encouragement, and short confetti animation.
  • Clean modern styling, nature and fresh-air imagery, and a motivational tone.
  • A build that can be demonstrated in a short video and shared in a public GitHub repository. Deployment remains a learning goal; hosting is chosen during technical planning.
Later · useful extensions
  • Money-saved calculations.
  • Researched and sourced information about quitting products, including the originally mentioned pouches, gum, and patches.
  • A broader health information library.
  • A more extensive quit plan and broader healthy-lifestyle features.
Cut · no permanent exclusions

No additional features were permanently rejected. The larger ideas are deferred so this first experiment stays manageable.
